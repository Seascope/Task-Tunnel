# Task Tunnel Play Review Video Script

Record a short, continuous, factual demonstration on the release candidate. Keep Android's taps and resulting state changes visible. Do not edit the video in a way that obscures the permission sequence.

1. Launch Task Tunnel with fresh app data. Show **Keep the reason you opened the app** before any Android permission screen.
2. Advance to **A quiet layer over the apps you already use**. Briefly show the three user choices on intervention: Return, Allow anyway, and End Tunnel.
3. Advance to **Why Task Tunnel needs Accessibility access**. Pause long enough to show what the service can see, why it is needed, what is and is not stored, local processing, and the digital-wellbeing purpose.
4. Tap **Continue to Accessibility settings**. Emphasize that this is an affirmative user action and that settings were not opened automatically at startup.
5. In Android Accessibility settings, enable **Task Tunnel protection**. Return to Task Tunnel.
6. Show **Protection is ready**, finish setup, then open Protection and show **Protection active**.
7. Open Instagram. At the Purpose Gate choose **Reply to messages**.
8. Enter Messages and show that normal use continues without an overlay.
9. Enter Reels. Show the Task Tunnel intervention and its explanation that Reels is outside the selected purpose.
10. Tap **Return** and show the app moving back. Repeat if needed and tap **Allow anyway** to demonstrate the temporary user override. Do not imply that the app prevents all bypasses.
11. Return to Task Tunnel and briefly show the resulting local Attention episode/timeline.
12. Open Protection and show Instagram/YouTube intentions, optional Drift configuration, installed versions in production-safe diagnostics, and permission health.
13. Open Settings > **Privacy and Accessibility explanation** and **Protection diagnostics**. Copy the report and show that it contains technical state only.
14. Show **Clear Attention history**, stop at the confirmation unless the reviewer needs the completed clear behavior.

Before submission, verify the sequence and wording against the live Play Console Accessibility declaration and current Google Play policy. This storyboard prepares evidence; it does not guarantee approval.
